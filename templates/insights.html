FeaturesTF-IDF, 1 to 2 word groups, 20,000 max
Train / test split80% / 20% (487 test reviews)

Hybrid approach for short reviews

A TF-IDF model has very little to work with in tiny reviews like “this is good” or “average”. So the system combines two methods:

1. Short reviews (8 words or fewer): if a built-in sentiment word list disagrees with the model, the word list decides.
2. Longer reviews: the ML model decides. The word list only steps in when the model is unsure (confidence below 55%) and the words are very clear.
3. Negations (“not good”) and intensifiers (“very good”) are handled. The result page shows which method decided.
